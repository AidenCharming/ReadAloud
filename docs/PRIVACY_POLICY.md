## The short version

ReadAloud keeps your books, scans, bookshelf and lending notes **on your phone**. There is no account, no advertising
and no analytics. The app sends information over the internet only in the cases listed below.

## What stays on your phone

* Books you import or scan, and your place in them.
* Your bookshelf: titles, authors, ratings, notes, and the names and dates of books you have lent to friends.
* Your voice choice and reading speed.

Nothing in this list is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also copy your shelf to a
file yourself (Bookshelf, menu, "Back up shelf to a file"); where that file goes is up to you.

## What goes over the internet

| When | What is sent | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| You scan a barcode or cover, or add a book | The ISBN, or the title and author text you search for | Open Library (openlibrary.org) |
| A cover is shown | The address of that cover image | Open Library (covers.openlibrary.org) |
| You download a voice | A normal file request, including your IP address | GitHub (github.com release downloads) |

Scanning pages, reading cover text and scanning barcodes use Google Play services (ML Kit). They run on your device;
the page photos and the text read from them are not sent by ReadAloud anywhere. Google's own handling of Play
services is described in Google's privacy policy.

## Camera, notifications and storage

* **Camera:** ReadAloud does not hold camera permission. Scanning is done through Google's scanner screens, which ask you
  to allow the camera themselves.
* **Notifications:** used for the lock-screen player and for reminders that a lent book is due back.
* **Files:** the app only opens a file you choose.

## Children

ReadAloud is not aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
